Privacy policy

Effective date: November 23, 2026

SunPitch is a measuring tool that runs on your iPhone. It doesn't need to know who you are, and it doesn't send what you measure to us.

The short version

  • There is no account and no sign-in.
  • We don't collect personal data. The app has no analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs, and it doesn't track you across apps or websites.
  • The camera and your location are processed on your iPhone and never sent to us.
  • Your projects, readings, photos and reports stay on your device.
  • Apple handles every purchase. We never see your payment details.

Who we are

This policy covers the SunPitch iPhone app and this website. SunPitch is developed and published by QIDIENT GLOBAL LTD (“we”, “us”), the seller shown on the SunPitch App Store page.

What we collect

Nothing. SunPitch has no account, and we don't run a server that the app talks to. The app contains no analytics, advertising, attribution or crash-reporting SDKs, doesn't use the advertising identifier and never asks to track you. We don't sell or share personal information.

The app keeps a few counters on your iPhone so it can run itself, for example whether you finished setup. They never leave the device.

Camera

SunPitch asks for camera access when you first use a feature that needs it: the camera pitch estimate, a sun sight or eave sight, and the sun path drawn over the live view.

  • Camera frames are processed in memory on your iPhone and are never uploaded.
  • Only images you choose to keep, such as a captured camera estimate or a sun-path snapshot, are stored, inside the app on your device.
  • SunPitch saves an image to your Photos library only when you tap Save Image. It asks for add-only access, so it can't see the rest of your library.

Location

SunPitch asks for your location while you use the app. It uses it on your iPhone to work out where the sun is, to find your roof on the map and to check the magnetic field against the World Magnetic Model, which is built into the app.

Your location is never sent to us. Coordinates are kept only with projects you save, on your device.

The pitch gauge uses the phone's motion sensors, which work on the device and need no permission.

Apple Maps

Satellite imagery for eave sight and address search come from Apple Maps. When you view the map or search for an address, your iPhone sends that request directly to Apple, and Apple's privacy policy applies. SunPitch also asks Apple's geocoding service for the time zone at your location, so sun times are shown in local time. We don't receive these requests or their results.

Data on your device

Everything you enter or create stays in the app's own storage on your iPhone. That includes projects, addresses, roof faces, pitch readings, calibrations, sun-path snapshots, notes, material profiles, your company name and logo for reports, the first name you can type during setup, and your settings.

  • SunPitch 1.0 doesn't sync between devices.
  • If you back up your iPhone to iCloud or a computer, the backup can include SunPitch data. Those backups are controlled by you and Apple.
  • Delete a project in the app to remove it. Deleting the app removes all of its data from the device.

Sharing and exports

When you share a PDF report or a share card, SunPitch hands the file to the iOS share sheet and you choose where it goes, such as Messages, Mail or Files. The privacy policy of the app or person you send it to applies from there. SunPitch keeps no copy outside your device.

Purchases

SunPitch Pro is sold through the App Store. Apple processes the payment, and we never receive your card details, name or email address. The app checks your subscription status on your iPhone with Apple's StoreKit. Apple gives us sales and subscription reports that don't identify you.

Crash reports

If you have chosen to share analytics with app developers in your iPhone's Privacy & Security settings, Apple may send us anonymous crash reports and usage statistics for SunPitch. Apple collects these, not the app. You can turn this off at any time in the same settings.

Emails to support

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you include in the message. We use it only to answer you, and we delete it when it's no longer needed for that.

This website

This website is a set of static pages. It sets no cookies and loads no analytics, tracking scripts, advertising or third-party fonts. Our hosting provider may keep standard request logs, such as IP address, browser type and time of visit, to deliver the pages and keep them secure. We don't use them to identify you.

Children

SunPitch is a professional tool for adults and isn't directed at children under 13, or the minimum age in your country. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children. The app doesn't collect personal information from anyone.

Your choices and rights

  • Turn camera, location or Photos access on or off at any time in your iPhone's Settings, under SunPitch. Features that need a permission ask again when you use them.
  • Because we don't hold personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct, export or delete on our side. The data on your iPhone is under your control.
  • Wherever you live, including the EU, the UK and California, you can contact us with any privacy question and we will answer it.

Changes to this policy

If we change how SunPitch handles data, we'll update this page and its effective date before the change ships. If a future version ever collects data, for example to sync projects through iCloud, we'll say so here, in the release notes and in the App Store privacy details first.

Contact

Questions about privacy: support@qidientlabs.com
