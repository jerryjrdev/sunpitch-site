Measurements and estimates

SunPitch is a measuring aid. Read the methodology and accuracy page for how each number is produced and how close it gets.

  • Camera pitch results are estimates. They depend on seeing the roof edge square-on, lens distortion and where you place the line.
  • Sun paths, sun positions, best-tilt targets and panel alignment are estimates for planning. They are only as accurate as the heading calibration under them.
  • No output from SunPitch is a bankable production report, an energy, savings or financing figure, a system design or a substitute for a professional survey or inspection.
  • Material counts in the takeoff come from the coverage values in your material profile. Check them against the manufacturer's wrapper.
  • Check critical measurements yourself before you cut material, place an order or quote a price.

Safety on roofs

  • Placing the phone on a roof is for trained roofers using fall protection. Set it down only when you are tied off and stable.
  • Never use SunPitch while walking on a roof, a ladder or any surface where you could fall.
  • Homeowners: stay off the roof. Use the camera estimate from a safe spot, or measure a rafter in the attic.
  • Wet, icy or frosty surfaces, loose granules and wind make any roof more dangerous. When in doubt, measure another day.
  • When sighting the sun, look at the screen, never at the sun.

You are responsible for your own safety and for following the safety rules that apply to your work and location.
